Chapter 10
How to Start

How to Start — Your 6-Step Digital Journey

You do not need much money or a large investment. You do not need followers. You need clarity and action. Here is exactly what to do — step by step.

01

Choose ONE Skill to Learn First

  • Pick ONE from: Digital Marketing, Content Creation, or Affiliate Marketing
  • Do not try to learn all of them at once — depth beats breadth at the start
  • Commit 60–90 days of focused learning before moving to the next skill
02

Learn From a Structured Source

  • Random YouTube videos give concepts but not strategy or a complete system
  • Invest in a proper course that gives you step-by-step guidance and community
  • iDigitalpreneur courses (by Divyanshi) give you exactly this — with mentorship
03

Build Your Personal Brand

  • Start one social media account around your niche — Instagram or YouTube
  • Post consistently for 90 days even if you have zero followers at the start
  • Your personal brand is your most valuable long-term digital asset
04

Create Your First Income Stream

  • Apply your skill as a freelancer or start affiliate marketing immediately
  • Your first Rs. 5,000 earned online will change how you see money forever
  • Reinvest early earnings into better tools or marketing — not lifestyle yet
05

Grow Your Audience and Build Trust

  • Share real value — your knowledge, your journey, your honest results
  • Trust takes time but compounds — 1,000 loyal followers beats 100,000 random ones
  • Engage with comments, reply to DMs, show up as a genuine human being
06

Scale — Courses, Consulting, Passive Income

  • Once you have skills + audience + income, create your own course or coaching offer
  • Add multiple affiliate streams and stack passive income sources
  • Now you have financial freedom — not just a job
